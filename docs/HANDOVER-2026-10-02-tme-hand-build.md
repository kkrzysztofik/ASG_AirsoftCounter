## Environment / commands

Run everything from `hardware/`. Use `/usr/bin/python3` (the `pcbnew` bindings are only in the system
Python). `rtk` rewrites output; use plain `make`, `/usr/bin/grep -F` and `diff` when you need exact text.

- Build one board: `make BOARD=<board> all` (sch -> netlist parity -> pcb -> autoroute -> ERC -> DRC -> fab).
  `<board>` is one of `carrier pack pack_b lipo carrier_tme pack_tme lipo_tme`.
- Faster loop while iterating on placement: `make BOARD=<board> pcb` then
  `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all -o build/<board>_drc.rpt <board>.kicad_pcb`.
  On an unrouted board only courtyard/silk/parity matter; unconnected-pad reports are expected.
- Schematic only: `make BOARD=<board> sch`; a failed layout writes `build/<board>_bad.kicad_sch`
  and prints the clash list.
- Every board module self-checks: `/usr/bin/python3 <board>.py`. The JLC originals must stay green:
  `for b in carrier pack pack_b lipo; do BOARD=$b /usr/bin/python3 -c "from board import design; design.check()"; done`
- Toolchain in use: kicad-cli 9.0.8, pcbnew 9.0.8, freerouting-2.4.1.jar, openjdk 25, GNU make.

## The blocker: T5.2 (pack schematic/PCB)

`gen_sch` cannot route `pack_tme`. `BOARD=pack_tme /usr/bin/python3 gen_sch.py` fails with an
`IndexError` in `name_label` (an empty-routed one-block net). Diagnostics from a monkeypatched
`gen_sch.astar`/`route`:

- Draft 1 — four INA228s in one row in block 1 (spacing 35.56 mm, tags at y 137.16): 7 router
  failures (`SDA_INT`, `SCL_INT` from the INA228 row; `NRST`, `BOOT0`, `SCL_EXT`, `MON_ALERT` from the
  MCU block) and one empty-routed net.
- Draft 2 — 2x2 INA228 grid at 50 mm spacing, tags moved to y 158.75: still at least one empty-routed
  net and the same `name_label` crash.
- The failing starts always have open neighbours, so it is the **goal region** that is unreachable, not
  the pin's escape: usually the pull-up pins in block 1 or the MCU tree. The relevant symbol change is
  that `MCU_ST_STM32L0:STM32L072KZTx` is 25.4 mm wide (pins at x +/-15.24) versus the C071's 15.24 mm
  (pins at +/-10.16), which shifts every MCU pin's escape corridor. The INA228 (via INA226) symbol is
  ~20 x 25 mm.

Both drafts were reverted; `hardware/pack_tme.py` is at the T5.1 commit and still self-checks. The
T5.2 edits are in git's reflog if you want them, but they do not work.

**What to do:** design the pack `BLOCKS`/`PLACE` fresh around the wider L072 symbol and the four
INA228s. The most promising direction is to put each INA228 in the **cell block it serves** (next to
that cell's shunt) instead of four in one block, which also matches the plan's intent for the PCB
("each next to its cell's shunt pair, Kelvin traces from the 10R split"). The carrier TME block (see
`carrier_tme.py` `BLOCKS[1]`) shows the pattern that finally routed: 25.4 mm part spacing, explicit
global labels for every internal net, and `"wired": {...}` + tags for rails whose pins are 2.54 mm
apart. Also note `gen_sch` writes `build/<board>_bad.kicad_sch` and prints the clash list on failure —
use it as the oracle instead of guessing.

## PCB constraints for T5.2 (from the plan, all still relevant)

- 96 x 90 mm, holders on the bottom, SMD on top, unchanged outline and holes. The four 18650 holders
  cover roughly x 8-85 in each cell band, so **only x < 8 and x > 85 are holder-free**.
- `J_USB1` (GCT USB4085, through-hole) must not sit over a holder and keeps its board-edge position
  (`_STRIP["J_USB1"] = (90, 23, 90)`). Its footprint has **no NPTH** and 0.4 mm signal drills, so
  `PTH_COMPONENT_MIN` must be lowered to 0.4 and the USB shell entries must be dropped from
  `NPTH_EXPECT`. The committed `pack_tme.py` already does this.
- The BQ25601 hand-solder footprint is 1.2 mm larger; nothing within 1 mm of its pad ends, and its
  back-side exposed-pad hole must not be under a holder (it is soldered from the back). The right strip
  (`x > 85`) is the only holder-free place big enough for the 7.7 mm footprint; the left margin is only
  8 mm wide. The committed `pack_tme.py` moves `U_CHG` to `(90, 36)` and the THT tact switches to
  `(4, 58)`/`(4, 66)` for this reason.
- `BOTTOM = {BT1..4, TH1..4}` (holders and bead NTCs are bottom-side).

## Rulings and decisions (all carried in the ledger, with their cost)

- **R_INP = R_INN = 240 k** (carrier amp gain). PAM8302A differential gain is
  `20 log(150k / (10k + R_IN))`; the DAC's full-scale differential is 2 x 2.1 Vrms = 4.2 Vrms, the BTL
  swing at VDD 3.6 V is ~2.55 Vrms, so the gain must be <= 0.607 -> R_IN >= 237 k. TME symbol
  `SMD0805-240K-1%`.
- **Tact switch = Omron B3F-1000** (6x6 mm THT, 4.5x6.5 mm pitch), which matches
  `Button_Switch_THT:SW_PUSH_6mm`. The plan named no symbol.
- **L1 = Bourns SRN6045-4R7Y** (TME `SRN60454R7Y-BOU-0`, Isat 4 A) on the existing `LRN6045`
  footprint; the plan's guess `SRN6045TA-4R7M` is not in the TME catalogue.
- **lipo_tme keeps `NOT_ASSEMBLED`** (the plan said `set()`). `gen_sch` marks the `TestPoint`/
  `SolderWire` symbols `in_bom=no` from the library and `gen_pcb` only honours `DNP|NOT_ASSEMBLED`, so
  an empty set produced 7 `footprint_symbol_mismatch` parity errors. Same applies to pack_tme, which
  keeps variant A's set minus `TH_CHG` (now bought at TME).
- **`tme.py` imports `board` as a module** and reads `board.design` inside `main()`; the form-import
  ran during `board`'s own import and cycled.
- **Carrier amp block** (this one took many iterations; read `carrier_tme.py` before touching it):
  every DAC/amp net has an explicit global label, `+3V3`/`GND` are `"wired"` in that block (the DAC's
  three supply pins are 2.54 mm apart and cannot each take a power port - `port_comb` only combines
  horizontally facing pins), the block sits at `(403.86, 12.7)`, and the 17 new parts' reference text is
  hidden. PCB: DAC/amp passives were placed with a courtyard-and-silk free-space search, 6-22 mm from
  `U_AMP`.
- **lipo_tme C_MCU3/C_VDDA sit ~11 mm from U_MCU** (the LQFP-32 corner is fully occupied), found by the
  same free-space search.
- **`pack_tme` adds only C_MON3/C_MON4**: the plan re-added C_MON1/C_MON2 that variant A already wires
  (caught by `check_structure`'s duplicate-pin rule).
- **QFN paste apertures over the exposed pad are kept** (`pcbnew.FootprintRemove`/`Remove` segfaults,
  and F.Paste is unused without a stencil). **`FootprintSave` returns `None`** in this SWIG build, so
  `handsolder_qfn.build()` asserts the file is non-empty instead.
- **TME stock**: INA228AIDGSR and PCM5100APWR showed 0 in stock on 2026-10-02 and the USB4085 16346.
  Stock is checked at order time (per the design doc).

## Environment gotchas (save yourself the time)

- **TME blocks curl (HTTP 403)** and its search page is JS-rendered, so neither curl nor a plain fetch
  sees results. What worked: `tme.eu` product-detail pages are indexed by Exa, so a
  `web_search` with `domainFilter: ["tme.eu"]` returns pages that show the `TME Symbol:` field. The
  product URL shape is `https://www.tme.eu/en/details/<symbol-lower>/...`. Single `fetch_content` calls
  on detail URLs also work but rate-limit (HTTP 429) after a couple.
- **st.com is unreachable**; use a datasheet mirror or the vendor page. TI/Diodes/Microchip PDFs fetch
  fine with curl and `pdftotext -layout` greps.
- **`gen_sch` layout rules** that cost the most time: `name_label` needs a straight run >= the label
  length with free cells on both sides (short internal nets fail), `rail_ports` gives each pin its own
  power port unless the pins face the same side and are 2 cells apart (`port_comb`), and every part must
  be in exactly one block, inside that block's rectangle. Tags are claimed before routing and must land
  on free grid cells. `STEP` is 1.27 mm.
- The pi-lens hook blocks edits that leave unguarded `open()` or unsorted imports; `ruff check <file>`
  is the repo's linter and is clean on all the committed files. `pcbnew` and local sibling imports need
  `# pyright: ignore[reportMissingImports]`.
- `make BOARD=lipo_tme all` and `make BOARD=carrier_tme all` take a few minutes each (freerouting).
