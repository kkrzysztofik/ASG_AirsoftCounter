# TME Hand-Build Boards Implementation Plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Iron-solderable, TME-sourced versions of the carrier, pack A and LiPo boards
(`hardware/{carrier,pack,lipo}_tme.*`, `hardware/fab/<board>_tme/`), generated by the same flow as the JLC
boards, as specified in `docs/plans/2026-10-02-tme-hand-build-design.md`. Firmware is out of scope.

**Architecture:** Three new board modules derive from the originals by import and edit, exactly like
`pack_b.py` derives from `pack.py`. Each carries a `TME` table `(value, footprint) -> TME symbol` instead
of `LCSC` and an empty `assembled()` (nothing is machine-placed). `board.check_structure` gains the TME
rule; the Makefile skips `jlc.py` for `*_tme` boards and `tme.py` writes their Quick Buy list straight
from the module. One project footprint is new: the BQ25601 QFN-24 with iron-friendly pads.

**Tech stack:** Python 3 (`/usr/bin/python3`, needed for `pcbnew`), KiCad 9 (`kicad-cli`, pcbnew API),
Freerouting 2.4.1 (`hardware/tools/`), GNU make. TME catalogue lookups through WebFetch.

**Conventions (read before starting):**
- Run everything from `hardware/`. Use `/usr/bin/python3`. `rtk` rewrites output; use plain `make`,
  `/usr/bin/grep -F`, `diff` when you need exact output.
- TME blocks curl (HTTP 403). Look symbols up with WebFetch on
  `https://www.tme.eu/pl/katalog/?queryPhrase=<part>` (the `/en/` path rate-limits sooner; URL-encode `/`
  as `%2F`). Every new TME symbol gets a comment `# <maker part>, <what it is> (TME 2026-10-xx)`.
- The originals (`design.py`, `pack.py`, `pack_b.py`, `lipo.py`) and their `fab/` output must not change,
  except the two small refactors named in T1.2 and T3.1. After every task run
  `for b in carrier pack pack_b lipo; do BOARD=$b /usr/bin/python3 -c "from board import design; design.check()"; done`.
- Commit after every task; end messages with the attribution line in use for this repo.
- Pin numbers below come from the KiCad 9 symbols, read 2026-10-02:
  - `MCU_ST_STM32L0:STM32L072KZTx` (LQFP-32): 1 VDD, 2 PC14, 3 PC15, 4 NRST, 5 VDDA, 6-13 PA0-PA7,
    14 PB0, 15 PB1, 16 VSS, 17 VDD, 18 PA8 (I2C3_SCL), 19 PA9, 20 PA10, 21 PA11 (USB_DM), 22 PA12 (USB_DP),
    23 PA13 (SWDIO), 24 PA14 (SWCLK), 25 PA15, 26 PB3, 27 PB4 (I2C3_SDA), 28 PB5, 29 PB6 (I2C1_SCL),
    30 PB7 (I2C1_SDA), 31 BOOT0 (dedicated pin, unlike the C071), 32 VSS.
  - `Sensor_Energy:INA228` (VSSOP-10): 1 A1, 2 A0, 3 ALERT, 4 SDA, 5 SCL, 6 VS, 7 GND, 8 VBUS, 9 IN-, 10 IN+.
  - `Audio:PCM5100A` (TSSOP-20): 1 CPVDD, 2 CAPP, 3 CPGND, 4 CAPM, 5 VNEG, 6 OUTL, 7 OUTR, 8 AVDD, 9 AGND,
    10 DEMP, 11 FLT, 12 SCK, 13 BCK, 14 DIN, 15 LRCK, 16 FMT, 17 XSMT, 18 LDOO, 19 DGND, 20 DVDD.
  - `Amplifier_Audio:PAM8302AAD` (SOP-8): 1 SD, 2 NC, 3 IN+, 4 IN-, 5 OUT+, 6 VDD, 7 GND, 8 OUT-.
  - `Regulator_Switching:MCP1640x-xCHY` (SOT-23-6): 1 SW, 2 GND, 3 EN, 4 VFB, 5 VOUT, 6 VIN.

    so the USB nets carry over unchanged.

## Changes against the design doc (record them in it in T0.1)

- **LiPo LDO is the SOT-23 AP7381-33SA-7**, not the SOT-89 AP7381-33Y-13: KiCad has a symbol only for the
  SOT-23 one, TME stocks it, and the LDO carries only the MCU (mA). Pinout 1 VI, 2 VO, 3 GND.
- **Amp is PAM8302AADCR (SOP-8)**, not AASCR: AAD is the 1.27 mm SOP-8, AAS the 0.65 mm MSOP-8. Confirm in T0.1.
- **The carrier link uses I2C1 (PB6/PB7) on both boards; the pack's internal bus uses I2C3 (PA8/PB4).**
  I2C1 is the port the ROM bootloader listens on (confirm in AN2606), so the Heltec could flash a pack or
  LiPo MCU over the cable later.
- **PCM5100A XSMT sits on AMP_SD**: the expander line that enables the amp also unmutes the DAC.
- **MCP1640 needs no catch diode** (synchronous): `D1` goes. FB divider 75k/24k -> 1.21 V x 4.125 = 4.99 V.

## Phase T2: the iron-friendly BQ25601 footprint

### Task T2.1: Generate `local.pretty/QFN-24-1EP_4x4mm_P0.5mm_EP2.6x2.6mm_HandSolder`

**Files:** Create `hardware/tools/handsolder_qfn.py`, `hardware/local.pretty/QFN-24-1EP_4x4mm_P0.5mm_EP2.6x2.6mm_HandSolder.kicad_mod`.

The script loads KiCad's `Package_DFN_QFN:QFN-24-1EP_4x4mm_P0.5mm_EP2.6x2.6mm`, and with pcbnew:
- every numbered SMD pad 1-24: lengthen by `EXT = 0.6` mm **outward only** (grow the pad's long side by EXT
  and move its centre outward by EXT/2, away from the footprint origin along that side's normal);
- pad 25 (exposed): becomes a plated through-hole pad, rect 2.6 x 2.6 on F.Cu/B.Cu/masks, drill
  `HOLE = 1.5` mm, so the pad is soldered from the back through the hole; drop the paste layer;
- courtyard grown by EXT on all sides; description says how it is meant to be soldered;
- saved with `pcbnew.FootprintSave` into `local.pretty`.

Self-check in the script (`__main__`): reload the saved footprint and assert 24 SMD pads each `EXT` longer
than the source, pad 25 PTH with drill 1.5 mm, pad-to-pad gap between neighbours unchanged, and the
extended pads' outer edge ~0.6 mm beyond the original. Run `/usr/bin/python3 tools/handsolder_qfn.py`,
expected `handsolder_qfn ok`. Open it in the footprint viewer once and look. Commit both files.
